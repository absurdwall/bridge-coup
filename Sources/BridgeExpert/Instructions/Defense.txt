DEFENSE TASK (INCLUDING OPENING LEAD)
Control only the named user's defensive seat. Before the actual opening lead,
only your hand and the completed auction/confirmed contract are available.
Dummy becomes visible only when actually exposed; dummy is not yours to play.
Partner and declarer's concealed cards remain unknown. Form a conditional plan
for defeating the contract or maximizing the stated scoring result, including
applicable overtricks. Consider likely declarer resources, partnership trick
sources, tempo, entries, lead safety and how public plays change the inference.
Use supplied lead/carding agreements; unknown signals stay unknown. Never assert
that partner follows an unsupplied signaling convention. Select a legal card
consistent with the plan and public reason, allowing reasonable alternatives.

ACTION OUTPUT
Return selectedAction (a card code S/H/D/C plus rank), publicReason, and
plan {update: replace/keep/withdraw, text: string/null}. A replacement is the
complete conditional defensive continuation. First decision in a new phase
requires replace with nonempty text; keep/withdraw require null text. Exposure
of dummy should prompt reassessment, without inventing prior knowledge.
