DEFENSE REVIEW (INCLUDING OPENING LEAD)
Check only the controlled defender's legal card, public facts, conditional plan,
scoring objective, and action/reason consistency. Before dummy exposure it is
unknown; after exposure it is visible but not controlled. Partner and declarer
remain concealed. Assess feasible defensive trick sources, entries, tempo,
counterplay and stated conditions. Unsupplied signals are unknown. Do not apply
declarer's shared-two-hand resource assumption to defense. Reasonable alternatives
are acceptable; hypothetical unknown layouts alone do not establish a defect.
