Source idea, briefly paraphrased: 3NT South, West leads SK. Delay SA until the third spade round, then establish diamonds, hoping the defender with DA can no longer reach the long spades. Source scoring and vulnerability are unspecified.

Independent reasoning for the adapted fixture:

- Five immediate cashing tricks are SA, three heart tricks, and CA. Although the partnership has HA/K/Q/J, both hands have only three hearts; the honors cannot produce four separate heart tricks. Four diamond winners are available after DA is driven out.
- With actual West five spades and East three, two ducks consume East's spades. Winning the third round with SA then losing a diamond to East leaves no direct spade return. The diamond length and HA entry support cashing four established diamonds.
- Taking SA early permits East to return a remaining spade after winning DA, so the chosen legal defensive fixture can actually exercise hold-up timing. A weak opponent that instead gives away tricks must be assessed on those actual events, and does not prove the specialized hold-up skill was tested.
- The live player cannot know East's three-spade length or DA merely because they occur in the fixture. Even after each defender has followed to three spade rounds, two unseen spades remain among defenders; East being void is still a hypothesis without additional evidence. Do not grade a confident “East is now known void” as factual at that time.
- Low spade spot choices within the relevant holding are not unique labels. Sensible conditional planning, lawful following, retained controls, recognition of actual switches and explicit assumptions matter. A differing legal line needs its own concrete adjudication; do not auto-fail it for disagreeing with the published demonstration.

The source itself warns that hold-up can be wrong when a switch is more dangerous. No generic “always duck twice” rule should enter prompts or rubric.

## Pavlicek Example 1: legal full deal; fix information time

Exact holdings in S/H/D/C order:

| Seat | Holding |
|---|---|
| North | `653 / 43 / KQ975 / AT4` |
| East | `K2 / QT98 / AJ8 / J852` |
| South | `AQ74 / AK62 / 43 / K73` |
| West | `JT98 / J75 / T62 / Q96` |

Independent counting confirms 13 each and 52 unique cards. Source demonstration starts `W:SJ, N:S3, E:SK, S:SA`. At the *end* of that actual trick South has six sure tricks: SQ, HA, HK, CA, CK, and the already-won SA. Before East actually plays SK, SQ is not an unconditional top winner when auction and lead methods are absent. Prefer the teaching short scenario at South's next lead, with that completed trick explicitly in history. A full board may start immediately after SJ, but its initial rubric must respect this earlier information time.

Source idea, briefly paraphrased: lose a low diamond early to preserve communications with the long diamond suit. The demonstrated deep finesse uses D9 before leading toward a higher diamond honor.

Independent reasoning:

- South starts with only two diamonds and North's sole reliable outside entry is CA. The plan must establish three diamond tricks while preserving a route to their cashing hand.
- Demonstration: South low diamond; West low; North D9; East DJ. After a return won in South, lead the remaining diamond toward DK. Against the actual AJ8/T62 layout the two high defender honors can be removed over the first two/three rounds while CA remains available. North can cash three established diamonds.
- Contrast: playing to DK immediately lets East duck with a low diamond. South's second diamond has been consumed if the suit is continued from North; using CA prematurely can leave the remaining long diamonds stranded. That is a concrete communication defect, not a requirement to imitate exact spot cards.
- Accept equivalent low South diamond choices, justified early concession/deep-finesse lines, and alternatives that demonstrate sufficient actual entries. Do not reveal East's AJ8 or West's T62 to the playing model, or treat either as known from the initial lead.
- Arrange legal opponents to preserve the entry/timing problem. If a defender gratuitously takes DA early or gives an extra entry, revise assessment to the resulting visible position; do not claim the original specialty was proven.

Neither candidate establishes a single-dummy unique best action. Complete hidden deals serve the environment and legality checks.
