## What to build

Freeze and evaluate the complete Sol Medium player/reviewer pipeline through the production public entry, producing a reproducible Stage 1 conclusion based on delivered advice and play rather than drafts or reviewer approval.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Acceptance freezes the implementation, both instruction sources, both role configurations, native runtime/capture code, fixtures and rubrics; preserve each candidate and original failures.
- [ ] Evaluator binds actual delivered outputs to their true initial/revision request, authorized current position and execution/plan association, without pretending a feedback envelope is byte-identical to the original snapshot.
- [ ] Retain the independent acceptance grader; do not feed its rubric answers or retrospective feedback into live play or use runtime approval as proof of quality.
- [ ] Run six short scenarios three times and two complete notrump boards twice (18 short plus four full-board attempts), without unplanned rerolls or mixing versions. Repaired drafts are diagnostic; actual serious errors, incompleteness and unresolved grades do not silently pass.
- [ ] Preserve the original material coverage and serious-error standard. Keep legal execution, contract result and bridge judgment separate, including contested executions.
- [ ] Report every full-board duration and first/later decision distributions, role costs, feedback rounds, technical retries, tokens, stop outcomes and finite-coverage limits. No three-minute speed gate is added.
- [ ] Complete relevant offline/regression tests and independent standards/spec code review, repair evidenced defects and revalidate affected evidence within the amendment's working budget.
- [ ] The final report explicitly identifies met/unmet conditions; no UI work, merge or acceptance-issue closure is implied by passing unit tests or finishing a report.
