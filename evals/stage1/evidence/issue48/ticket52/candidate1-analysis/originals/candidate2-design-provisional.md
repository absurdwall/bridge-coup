## Minimal instruction changes to consider after full candidate-1 review

Preserve the jointly approved planning framework and current instruction layout.
Prefer a few local clarifications inside existing guidance/process and keep
semantics, not a second planning pipeline, output checklist, mandatory public
reasoning transcript, policy DSL or accumulating list of prohibited cards.
Exact edits require writing-for-agents review and versioned diff/hashes before
another freeze. The following are **generic editing objectives**, not instructions
currently sent to either role:

1. **Player, existing route/transition guidance:** make clear that a named
   remainder follows subtraction of every card spent in the proposed sequence,
   including losing tricks, follows and unblockings. Separate tricks already won,
   total source yield and winners still reachable after the next entry. Trace
   necessary concessions through control recovery and access to the cashing hand.
   This strengthens the existing instruction to connect phases without adding
   arrays, additional output fields or a mandated exhaustive tree.
2. **Player, current-card reconciliation / keep definition:** assess the
   unfinished applicable plan after the selected card is hypothetically consumed.
   If that card removes a critical explicitly reserved resource/transfer or a
   different key sequence is now needed, provide a complete replacement rather
   than correcting only publicReason. Already-completed phases and eliminated
   conditional branches remain historical; do not replace just to erase them.
3. **Both roles, existing material-counterplay check:** a quantitative guarantee
   must match its specified play rule under material legal defensive responses,
   including spot cards as well as honors where they determine that rule. Narrow
   an unsupported claim or supply an executable conditional adjustment. This is
   verification of what the proposal actually promises, not a requirement to
   guarantee the contract against every unseen layout or attain optimal play.
4. **Reviewer, existing remaining-route/current-action assessment:** independently
   check the same post-sequence inventory/entry and post-current-card consistency
   of proposedEffectivePlan. A different feasible current reason does not fix an
   explicitly impossible still-applicable kept route. Established defects in
   counts, critical resources or entry sequence belong in revise, even if the
   selected card is legal or a later adaptation could rescue the result. Preserve
   the actual verdict/evidence and existing uncertainty/technical policies.

Implementation should avoid duplicating all four objectives verbatim in every
section or role. They can usually be expressed as two short player changes and
one coherent reviewer clarification adjacent to the current responsibilities.
No specific card/rank, suit diagram, reference sequence, expected answer, failed
output quotation, rubric answer or retrospective proof should enter live
instructions. In particular, do not bake in the demonstrated spot, long-suit
spot winner or heart-transfer honor as hints. Those belong only in evaluator
regression evidence.

### Preserve routine flexibility rather than forcing needless replacements

Short-6-r3 is the positive control: “cash South's heart honors before using that
entry when practical,” together with explicit HA preservation, permits two
honor wins and overlap of the last honor with HA. Actual routine rank choice
retains the same entry purpose/count and the plan never fixes a now-unavailable
last card. It should continue to pass. Short-6-r2's explicitly reserved crossing
also remains available because its current choices actually preserve that card.

Contrast an explicit critical named sequence whose last card was already spent,
where the current reason proposes a materially different unfinished sequence.
Replacement is appropriate there. Do not require a replacement for every spot
swap, honor interchange, concise omitted obvious crossing, already completed
step or now-false conditional premise. This distinction protects the accepted
quality standard from both permissive silent repair and unnecessary review
blocking.
